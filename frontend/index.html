About VEMU IT

VEMU Institute of Technology provides an educational environment for students to develop their knowledge and skills. CampusGo is designed to support convenient and organized college transportation management.

Students can access transportation information through one platform, while administrators and drivers can manage their assigned responsibilities.

How to Register

  1. Open the CampusGo website.
  2. Select Register from the navigation bar.
  3. Enter the required student details.
  4. Submit your registration information.
  5. Log in after your account is registered and available for use.
